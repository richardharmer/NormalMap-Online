Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about our normal map generator and 3D texture creation.

基础问题 (Basic Questions)

Q: 什么是法线贴图(Normal Map)?
A: 法线贴图是一种纹理贴图,用于在不增加模型几何复杂度的情况下为3D表面添加细节。它通过存储表面法线方向信息来模拟凹凸和细节,是现代游戏开发中必不可少的技术。
Q: What is a normal map generator?
A: A normal map generator is a tool that creates normal maps from height maps, textures, or photos. Normal maps add surface detail to 3D models without increasing geometry complexity, making them essential for games and 3D graphics. Our online normal map generator creates high-quality results instantly in your browser.
Q: 法线贴图如何工作?
A: 法线贴图通过改变光照计算中的表面法线方向来工作。每个像素的RGB值代表法线向量的X、Y、Z分量。蓝色通道(Z)通常占主导,因为大多数法线指向表面外部。

使用问题 (Usage Questions)

Q: How to make a normal map from texture or height map?
A: To make a normal map:
  1. Upload your texture or height map (grayscale works best)
  2. Adjust the strength (2.0-3.0 recommended) and blur settings
  3. Preview the result in real-time 3D viewer
  4. Download your normal map as PNG or TGA
Our generator supports common formats like PNG, JPG, and TGA files.
Q: How do I use this normal map generator for Unity?
A: For Unity normal maps:
  1. Upload your height map to our generator
  2. Set the strength between 2-4 for best results
  3. Ensure "Invert G" is unchecked (Unity uses OpenGL format)
  4. Download as PNG format
  5. In Unity, import the texture and set the texture type to "Normal Map"
Q: 如何在Unreal Engine中使用法线贴图?
A: 在Unreal Engine中:
  1. 生成法线贴图时勾选"Invert G"(Unreal使用DirectX格式)
  2. 下载为PNG或TGA格式
  3. 在UE中导入,纹理类型会自动识别为Normal Map
  4. 在材质编辑器中连接到Normal输入
Q: Can I create normal maps online for free?
A: Yes! Our free normal map online generator converts any texture to a normal map online. Simply drag your texture file into the normal map online generator, adjust the intensity settings, and download the converted normal map online. Works with photos, hand-painted textures, and procedural textures. Everything runs in your browser with no uploads required.

技术问题 (Technical Questions)

Q: DirectX vs OpenGL法线贴图有什么区别?
A: 主要区别在于绿色通道(Y轴)的方向:
  • OpenGL格式: Y轴向上(Unity, Blender使用)
  • DirectX格式: Y轴向下(Unreal Engine使用)
在我们的工具中,勾选"Invert G"即可在两种格式间切换。
Q: What is the difference between tangent space and object space normal maps?
A:
  • Tangent Space: Most common, appears mostly blue/purple. Works with animated/deformed meshes. Our tool generates this type.
  • Object Space: Rainbow colored, only works with static meshes. Rarely used in modern games.
Q: 最佳的法线贴图分辨率是多少?
A: 推荐分辨率:
  • 移动游戏: 512x512 或 1024x1024
  • PC游戏: 2048x2048 或 4096x4096
  • 电影级: 4096x4096 或更高
使用2的幂次方尺寸(512, 1024, 2048等)以获得最佳性能。

高级功能 (Advanced Features)

Q: How to convert normal map to height map?
A: While our primary function is creating normal maps, you can approximate a height map from a normal map by:
  1. Using image editing software to extract the blue channel
  2. Applying blur to smooth the result
  3. Adjusting contrast for better definition
Note: This is an approximation and won't be as accurate as the original height map.
Q: How do I generate normal maps from photos (photogrammetry-style)?
A: Use our "Pictures" tab feature:
  1. Take 4 photos of the same surface with lighting from different directions (top, bottom, left, right)
  2. Upload each photo to the corresponding position in our tool
  3. Our algorithm calculates surface normals from lighting variations
  4. Adjust settings and download the result
Best results require consistent camera position (use a tripod) and controlled lighting.
Q: 如何批量处理多个法线贴图?
A: 使用批处理模式:
  1. 点击"Batch Mode"按钮
  2. 选择多个高度图文件
  3. 调整设置(将应用于所有文件)
  4. 选择要生成的贴图类型(Normal, Displacement, AO, Specular)
  5. 点击下载,所有贴图将自动生成并下载

隐私和安全 (Privacy & Security)

Q: Do my images get uploaded to any server?
A: No! This normal map generator runs entirely in your browser using WebGL technology. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security for your game assets and textures. All processing is done locally on your GPU.
Q: 我的数据安全吗?
A: 绝对安全!所有处理都在您的浏览器中本地完成,使用WebGL和GPU加速。我们不会:
  • 上传您的图片到服务器
  • 存储您的数据
  • 追踪您的文件
  • 收集个人信息

文件格式 (File Formats)

Q: What file formats are supported?
A: We support:
  • Input: PNG, JPG, JPEG, BMP, GIF, TGA, and most common image formats
  • Output: PNG (recommended), JPG, TGA, TIFF
For best results, use high-resolution grayscale or color images as input.
Q: 应该使用什么格式导出?
A: 推荐格式:
  • PNG: 无损压缩,最佳质量,Unity和UE都支持(推荐)
  • TGA: 无损,某些引擎首选
  • JPG: 有损压缩,文件小但质量降低(不推荐用于法线贴图)
  • TIFF: 专业用途,文件大

其他功能 (Other Features)

Q: Can I create displacement and AO maps too?
A: Absolutely! Our generator creates multiple map types from the same input:
  • Normal Maps: Surface detail simulation
  • Displacement Maps: Actual geometry displacement
  • Ambient Occlusion (AO) Maps: Soft shadows in crevices
  • Specular Maps: Highlight/glossiness control
Switch between different output types using the preview tabs.
Q: 如何创建无缝/可平铺的法线贴图?
A: 要创建无缝法线贴图:
  1. 确保输入的高度图本身是可平铺的
  2. 在图像编辑软件中使用"Offset"滤镜检查边缘
  3. 修复边缘不匹配的区域
  4. 在我们的工具中生成法线贴图
  5. 在3D预览中启用"Repeat"纹理模式验证

性能问题 (Performance)

Q: 为什么3D预览显示黑屏?
A: 如果3D预览区域显示黑屏,可能是因为:
  1. CORS问题: 直接打开HTML文件会导致浏览器安全限制。解决方法:使用本地HTTP服务器(如python -m http.server)
  2. WebGL不支持: 确保浏览器支持WebGL(Chrome、Firefox、Edge都支持)
  3. GPU驱动问题: 更新显卡驱动程序
Q: What browsers work best?
A: Recommended browsers:
  • Chrome: Best performance (recommended)
  • Firefox: Excellent support
  • Edge: Good performance
  • Safari: Works but slower
All browsers must support WebGL for the 3D preview to work.
